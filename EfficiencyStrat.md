# Efficiency Stratagem for UBIF Spring Hill
This document will highlight strategies for increasing productivity and efficiency at the Spring Hill location.

  - CX calling about in-progress phones
  - CX walking in to check in-progress phones
  - CX calling about multis
  - Lacking true roles
  - Questions by employees
  - Warranty on previous repair
  - OQC issues
  - Questions by CX
  - Broken by tech escalations

### CX & Multis

      Should be a singular number as CX only hear '3'
### Lacking True Roles
  <ins>***This one is important***</ins>  
  Only you can do everything needed to run the store, but that runs you ragged.
  Assigning *roles* to employees may increase their likelihood of starting and finishing multis.
  Yes we should all do HDMI's, but when it comes to advanced solder repairs it always escalates to you.
  You need to train us to become your limbs, not you. We do not have time to become as generally skilled as you.  
     
  <ins>Give each employee a role with advanced soldering and teach them a relatively short curriculum.</ins>   
  Examples:   
    - Trace Repair.   
    - IC Chips.  
    - Advanced Troubleshooting & Salvage [BoardRipper, multimeter, etc.]  
    - Non-HDMI ports  
      
  This will allow everyone to be interdependent AND we can teach other after enough time.  
  Trace repair and IC only share the foundational thinking, not skillset. There is no reason to teach on a 'perk unlock' system.  
  **<ins>This applies to general repair as well. The guys look at the shelf and get intimidated by the variety and choose the safe option.</ins>**  
  If you make Henok "the console kid" he will happily tackle a diagnosis and repair for a console with a strange issue.  
  If you make Daniel "Tablet Man" he will eventually build up more muscle memory and niche wisdom that he can impart upon others.  
  [I wanna be Laptop Laddy].  
  Yes, we should be doing ALL multidays but this system would help us move forward a bit more. I see people, including myself, pick something up and go "theres no way I'm allowed/able to do this"

## <ins>Intake Volume vs. Cash Balancing<ins/>
### Non-HDMI Solder
We charge way too little for non-HDMI solder repairs. Yes, I know the margins for them are extremely large but our customers don't know that. Did you know the largest margin on any item sold at a restaurant is the fountain drinks? Do we happily pay 4 dollars for a large Coke when it cost them 32 cents. The concept applies here. To customers, the item is bricked unless WE fix it. There is nobody nearby that can do these repairs. The same way that if I said your car's fuel pump is dead, you don't care about the margins youre gonna pay whatever sounds reasonable to fix it. It also allows us more rebate room for ATC.
