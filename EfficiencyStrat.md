# Efficiency Stratagem for UBIF Spring Hill
This document will highlight strategies for increasing productivity and efficiency at the Spring Hill location.

## Thesis: Culprits of efficiency decline
  - CX calling about in-progress phones
  - CX walking in to check in-progress phones
  - CX calling about multis
  - Lacking true roles
  - Questions by employees
  - Warranty on previous repair
  - OQC issues
  - Questions by CX
  - Broken by tech escalations

## Plans of Action
### CX & In-Progress Phones
  - Pagers, flip-phones, Meshtastic
      No longer getting up to check on a customer that is going to walk right back out.
      I am willing to pay for these out of my own pocket if it means not getting up for this again.
  - Security Monitoring
      We have the Ring already set up. We should easily be able to view when its just UPS
### CX & Multis
  - Longer quotes on TAT
  - 5 Days instead of 3-5
      Should be a singular number as CX only hear '3'
### Lacking true roles
  *This one is important*
  Only you can do everything needed to run the store, but that runs you ragged.
  Assigning *roles* to employees may increase their likelihood of starting and finishing multis.
